4.1 · Transversal Perspective

Technology succeeds only within a socio-technical system

Co-Determination Principle

Technology and organizational culture continuously shape each other. Modifying Systems (ICT) directly destabilizes Structure, Skills, and Leadership Style.

Resource & Knowledge-Based View

Software and hardware are commoditized. Sustainable competitive advantage lies in dynamic capabilities: integrating knowledge and adapting workflows.

Nolan's Maturity Curves

Maturity progresses from operational back-office automation to open data integration and organizational learning.

4.3 · Governance & Risk Perspective

AI Governance: Mitigating Shadow AI with ISO/IEC 42001

Operational IT Governance

COBIT 2019 · ITIL 4 · ISO 27001

  • Alignment of IT projects with business goals
  • Service level stability & incident management
  • CIA triad: Confidentiality, Integrity, Availability
Artificial Intelligence Management System

ISO/IEC 42001:2023 (AIMS)

  • Lifecycle controls for probabilistic & autonomous models
  • Training data lineage, traceability, and bias auditing
  • Compliance with legal mandates (e.g., EU AI Act)

Shadow AI Risk

Unregulated employee use of commercial LLMs leaks sensitive code and proprietary data.

38 Annex A Controls

Standardized operational controls turning ethical guidelines into certifiable enterprise practices.

Human-in-the-Loop

Continuous oversight to prevent algorithmic hallucination, discrimination, and autonomous error.

4.4 · Human & Social Perspective

Corporate Digital Responsibility (CDR) & Sustainability

Human Dimension

Cognitive Workplace Health

63% of remote workers switch daily across 4+ communications tools
Context Switching: Causes severe attention fragmentation, mental fatigue, and drops in deep work efficiency.
Hierarchical Flattening: AI absorbs middle-management analytical tasks, requiring restructured leadership paths.
Workforce Remedy: Workplace platform rationalization + structured enterprise-wide AI literacy.
Ecological Dimension

Green IT & Planetary Footprint

Scope 3 GHG Protocol indirect value-chain emissions in digital systems
AI Compute Surges: Massive electricity and cooling water demands driven by LLM training and cloud inferencing.
Digital Divide: MSMEs represent 95%+ of firms but face critical capital and knowledge barriers to adopt AI.
Green Strategy: Renewable cloud hosting SLAs, circular hardware lifecycles, and Scope 3 carbon audits.
The 7 CDR Principles: Purpose & Trust · Fair Access · Social Wellbeing · Economic Impact · Impact Economy · Planet · Green IT
